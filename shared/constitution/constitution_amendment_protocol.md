# Arqon Zero Constitution Amendment Protocol

Version: 0.1.0

## Required Flow

1. PM AI drafts amendment proposal.
2. Coder AI drafts amendment patch.
3. Helper/Codex applies patch and runs governance checks.
4. Auditor AI reviews the change.
5. Human ratifies or rejects.
6. Constitution version and manifest hashes are updated.

## Hard Constraints

An amendment must not:

- Remove human final promotion authority
- Permit AI self-certification
- Weaken sealed-test boundaries silently
- Apply retroactively to convert failed evidence into pass evidence
- Remove required conservative status language
- Convert hard fail classes into warnings without explicit human ratification
- Bypass audit replay requirements
